# Credit Card Fraud

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit2

## Definition
Credit card fraud in the mobile era involves the unauthorized use of credit card information, often obtained through hacking or technological manipulation, to perform fraudulent transactions.

## Key points
- **Techniques**: Phishing, tricky apps, triangulation, and credit card generators.
- **Prevention (Do's)**: Use strong PINs, secure WiFi, update apps, enable biometric locks, and notify the bank immediately if a card is lost.
- **Prevention (Don'ts)**: Do not save card info on websites, avoid public WiFi for transactions, and do not dispose of receipts in open trash.
- **Impact**: Financial loss, identity theft, and privacy invasion.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Triangulation | Fraudulent network of online transactions to resell stolen goods. |
| Phishing | Tricking users into revealing sensitive info via fake messages. |
