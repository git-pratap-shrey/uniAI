# Mobile and Wireless Devices

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit2

## Definition
Mobile and wireless devices are electronic gadgets, such as smartphones, tablets, and portable computers, that provide connectivity and computing capability in a small form factor, allowing users to access information and services while on the go.

## Key points
- **Rising Importance**: These devices act as "mini computers in your pocket," providing instant access to emails, internet, social media, and work tools.
- **Wireless Computing**: Refers to devices and networks that exchange data without physical cables (e.g., WiFi, Bluetooth, cellular networks).
- **Hand-held Devices**: Portable gadgets designed to be operated with one or both hands (e.g., Smartphones, PDAs, Ultra Mobile PCs).
- **Mobility Trends**: Includes physical, social/economic, technological, and transportation mobility; characterized by faster networking (4G/5G) and increased usability.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| PDA | Hand-held device for managing personal information and tasks. |
| UMPC | Compact, lightweight computer with a touchscreen. |
| Wireless Computing | Computing without physical connections like cables. |
