# Security Challenges of Mobile Devices

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit2

## Definition
Security challenges in mobile computing refer to the risks associated with taking information outside of physically controlled environments and granting remote access to protected corporate networks.

## Key points
- **Device-Level Risks**: Information is taken outside the office/home; devices are easily lost or stolen.
- **Technical Challenges**: Managing registry settings, authentication service security, cryptography, and LDAP security.
- **Remote Access**: Providing access to corporate networks from mobile devices creates entry points for attackers.
- **Unconventional Storage**: Use of stealth storage devices (e.g., USBs) can introduce malware into the network.
- **Software Risks**: Outdated software, lack of patching, and "jailbreaking" or "rooting" devices increase vulnerability.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Jailbreaking | Removing restrictions on iOS devices to expand capabilities. |
| Rooting | Removing restrictions on Android devices. |
| RAS | Remote Access Server; used for remote network access. |
