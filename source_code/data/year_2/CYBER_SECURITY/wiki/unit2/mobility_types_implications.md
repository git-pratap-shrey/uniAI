# Mobility Types and Implications

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit2

## Definition
Mobility refers to the ability of users, data, or devices to move easily from one place to another while maintaining connectivity and access to services.

## Key points
- **User Mobility**: Ability to access systems/data from various locations. Requires secure authentication.
- **Device Mobility**: Ability of devices to move between networks (e.g., WiFi to cellular) while maintaining uninterrupted connectivity.
- **Session Mobility**: Capability to transfer an ongoing network session (e.g., video call) from one device/network to another without interruption.
- **Service Mobility**: Ability to access the same services or applications across different devices or locations.
- **Implications**: Challenges include data synchronization, maintaining Quality of Service (QoS), and managing handoff mechanisms between networks.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Handoff | The process of switching between access points or networks. |
| Session Mobility | Transferring an active network session between devices. |
