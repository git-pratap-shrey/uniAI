# Chain of Custody

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit4

## Definition
The chain of custody is a documentary process that tracks the handling, location, and control of digital evidence from the moment of seizure until its presentation in court.

## Key points
- It acts as a 'special log' showing who touched the evidence, when, and why.
- Importance: Maintains evidence integrity, ensures legal admissibility, preserves the rights of the accused, and ensures accountability.
- If the chain is broken, evidence cannot be presented in a court of law.
- Documentation must include: Dates, times, locations, and identities of all individuals handling the evidence.
- Police role: Collect evidence, seal in bags with unique IDs, and maintain records of every transfer.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Chain of Custody | Chronological paper trail of evidence handling. |
| Admissibility | The quality of evidence being acceptable in a court of law. |

## Exam questions likely on this topic
