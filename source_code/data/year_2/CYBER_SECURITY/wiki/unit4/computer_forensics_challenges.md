# Computer Forensics Challenges

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit4

## Definition
Technical and legal obstacles that hinder the effective collection, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence in a forensic investigation.

## Key points
- Evolving Technology: Rapid advancements outpace the development of forensic tools.
- Encryption: Widespread use of encryption makes data access difficult.
- Anti-Forensics: Use of tools to erase or obfuscate digital trails (e.g., data hiding, steganography).
- Data Volume: Massive amounts of data make sifting for relevant evidence inefficient.
- Legal/Jurisdictional: Cross-border investigations and privacy concerns complicate legal compliance.
- Volatility: Digital evidence is easily altered; live systems are difficult to analyze without disruption.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Anti-Forensics | Techniques used to hide or destroy digital evidence. |
| Steganography | Hiding data inside other files (images/videos). |
| Data Volatility | The tendency of digital data to be easily lost or changed. |

## Exam questions likely on this topic
- Discuss the major technical challenges faced by forensic investigators.
- How do anti-forensic techniques impact a digital investigation?
