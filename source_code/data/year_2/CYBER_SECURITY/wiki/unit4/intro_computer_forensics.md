# Introduction to Computer Forensics

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit4

## Definition
Cyber forensic is a branch of forensic science which deals with the investigation, analysis technique on computer systems indulged in Cyber Crime, aiding in the collection and preservation of evidence for legal purposes.

## Key points
- Principles: Identifying, Preserving, Analysis, and Presenting.
- Digital forensic is the process of preservation, identification, extraction, and documentation of computer evidence for the court of law.
- It involves investigating digital media (computers, mobile phones, servers, NIC) to determine what happened and who is responsible.
- Importance: Produces evidence for court, ensures system integrity, protects organization assets, and tracks cyber criminals.
- Applications: Intellectual property theft, employment disputes, fraud investigations, and forgery.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Cyber Forensic | Investigation of computer systems involved in cyber crime. |
| Digital Forensic | Science of finding evidence from any digital media. |
| Dead Analysis | Forensic investigation performed on data at rest (hard disk). |
