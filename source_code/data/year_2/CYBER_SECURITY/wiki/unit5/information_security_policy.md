# Information Security Policy

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit5

## Definition
An information security policy is a formal document that outlines an organization's approach to protecting its information assets, defining the rules and procedures that employees must follow to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

## Key points
- Protects information assets from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.
- Helps organizations comply with applicable laws and regulations to avoid penalties.
- Reduces risks of financial losses, reputational damage, and accidental data loss.
- Creates a culture of security awareness and accountability within the organization.
- Key components include: statement of purpose, scope, asset classification, security controls, roles and responsibilities, and incident reporting procedures.
- Implementation steps: Assess risks, develop policy, communicate/train employees, monitor/enforce compliance, and review/update regularly.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Confidentiality | Ensuring information is accessible only to authorized users |
| Integrity | Maintaining the accuracy and completeness of data |
| Availability | Ensuring information is accessible when needed |

## Exam questions likely on this topic
