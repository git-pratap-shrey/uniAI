# Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit5

## Definition
The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 is a law of the Parliament of India to provide for the processing of digital personal data in a manner that recognizes both the right of individuals to protect their personal data and the need to process such data for lawful purposes.

## Key points
- Applies to processing digital personal data by organizations within India or providing goods/services to individuals in India.
- Defines "digital personal data" as data identifying an individual directly or indirectly (e.g., name, email, IP address, financial data).
- Individual rights: Access, correct, erase, object to processing, and port personal data.
- Organizational obligations: Obtain consent, implement reasonable security measures, notify of data breaches, and respond to user requests.
- Objectives: Protect privacy, promote responsible data processing, and establish a governance framework.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Digital Personal Data | Data about an individual who can be identified directly or indirectly |
| Data Breach | Unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction of personal data |
