# Indian Cyber Law

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit5

## Definition
Indian cyber law, formally known as the Information Technology Act, 2000 (ITA 2000) along with its amendments, is the primary legal framework governing cyberspace and electronic transactions in India.

## Key points
- Provides legal recognition to electronic documents, digital signatures, and electronic transactions.
- Addresses cybercrimes including hacking, identity theft, data breaches, malware, and cyber terrorism.
- Defines intermediary liability for content hosted on internet platforms.
- Establishes institutions like CERT-In (nodal agency for cyber security) and Cyber Appellate Tribunal (CAT).
- Prescribes penalties (fines and imprisonment) to deter illegal cyber activities.
- Rights of individuals: Privacy, access to information, free speech, and compensation for cybercrime victims.
- Rights of organizations: Intellectual property protection, business data protection, and legal action against cybercriminals.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| ITA 2000 | The primary legislation governing cyber activities in India |
| CERT-In | The nodal agency for responding to cyber security incidents |
| Intermediary | Platforms like social media or search engines hosting content |
