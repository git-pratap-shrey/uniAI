# Classification of Cybercrime

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit1

## Definition
Cybercrime classification refers to the grouping of illegal digital activities based on the target of the attack: individuals, property, organizations, or society.

## Key points
- **Against Individual**: Cyber stalking, spoofing, phishing, hacking, and defamation.
- **Against Property**: Credit card fraud, intellectual property (IP) crimes, internet time theft, and cyber squatting.
- **Against Organization**: Email bombing, salami attacks, logic bombs, Trojan horses, and data diddling.
- **Against Society**: Online gambling, cyber trafficking, forgery, cyber terrorism, and web jacking.
- Cyber stalking involves using the internet to harass or threaten an individual.
- Salami attacks involve making insignificant alterations to financial data to accumulate profit over time.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Cyber Squatting | Unauthorized registration of domain names similar to trademarks to profit in bad faith. |
| Data Diddling | Altering raw data before it is processed by a computer. |
| Web Jacking | Forcibly taking control of a website by cracking the password. |
