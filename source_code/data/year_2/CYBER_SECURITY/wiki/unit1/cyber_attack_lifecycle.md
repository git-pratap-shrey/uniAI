# Cyber Attack Lifecycle

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit1

## Definition
The Cyber Attack Lifecycle (or Kill Chain) describes the sequential stages a hacker follows to carry out a successful attack.

## Key points
- **Reconnaissance**: Gathering information about the target.
- **Weaponization**: Creating tools/malware to exploit weaknesses.
- **Delivery**: Sending the weapon to the target (e.g., email, USB).
- **Exploitation**: Triggering the vulnerability.
- **Installation**: Installing malware to maintain access.
- **Command and Control**: Establishing a channel for remote manipulation.
- **Actions on Objectives**: Achieving the final goal (e.g., data theft, disruption).

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Reconnaissance | The survey phase of gathering target information. |
| Attack Surface | The total sum of all possible entry points for an attack. |
