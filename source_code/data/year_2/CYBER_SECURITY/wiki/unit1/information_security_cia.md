# Information Security and CIA Triad

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit1

## Definition
Information Security is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.

## Key points
- **CIA Triad**:
  - **Confidentiality**: Ensuring data is accessible only to authorized users (e.g., Encryption).
  - **Integrity**: Ensuring data accuracy and completeness (e.g., Hashing, Digital signatures).
  - **Availability**: Ensuring data is accessible when needed (e.g., Backups, Redundant systems).
- **Types of Security**: Network, Application, Endpoint, Data, Cloud, Physical, and Governance.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Confidentiality | Protecting information from unauthorized disclosure. |
| Integrity | Protecting information from unauthorized modification. |
| Availability | Ensuring information is accessible to authorized users. |
