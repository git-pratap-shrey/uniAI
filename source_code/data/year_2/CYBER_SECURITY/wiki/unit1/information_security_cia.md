# Information Security and CIA Triad

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit1

## Definition
Information Security (InfoSec) is the practice of protecting information and systems from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.

## Key points
- **Confidentiality**: Ensuring information is accessible only to authorized users (e.g., encryption).
- **Integrity**: Ensuring accuracy and completeness of data (e.g., hashing, digital signatures).
- **Availability**: Ensuring authorized users have access when needed (e.g., backups, redundant systems).
- Types of security include Network, Application, Endpoint, Data, Cloud, and Physical security.
- The 5P Netizen Mantra for security: Precaution, Protection, Prevention, Preservation, and Perseverance.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| CIA Triad | The fundamental model of Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability. |
| Cryptography | Transforming information into an unreadable format to ensure security. |
| Incident Response | The approach to prepare for, detect, and recover from security incidents. |
