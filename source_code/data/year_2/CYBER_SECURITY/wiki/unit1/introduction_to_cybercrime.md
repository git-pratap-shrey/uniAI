# Introduction to Cybercrime

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit1

## Definition
Cybercrime is an unlawful act wherein the computer is either a tool, a target, or both.

## Key points
- Cybercrime involves criminal activity conducted using digital technology, computers, networks, or networked devices.
- It is also referred to as computer-related crime, e-crime, or high-tech crime.
- Challenges include the anonymity of perpetrators, the fact that many are well-educated, and the lack of awareness among victims regarding their cyber rights.
- Cybercrime is often categorized into crimes against individuals, property, organizations, and society.
- Traditional crime requires physical presence and physical tools, whereas cybercrime can be committed from any location with internet access.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Netizen | A citizen of the internet; someone actively involved in online communities. |
| Techno-crime | Premeditated acts against systems for financial gain or theft of information. |
| Techno-vandalism | Using technology to intentionally cause damage or disrupt systems without financial gain. |
