# Introduction to Cybercrime

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit1

## Definition
Cybercrime is an unlawful act wherein the computer is either a tool, a target, or both.

## Key points
- Cybercrime involves criminal activity conducted using digital technology, computers, or networks.
- It is also referred to as computer-related crime, internet crime, e-crime, or high-tech crime.
- Challenges include user unawareness, anonymity of attackers, and the fact that crimes are often committed by highly educated technical individuals.
- Cybercrime is classified into two types: Techno-crime (financial gain/theft) and Techno-vandalism (causing disruption/harm without financial motive).

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Netizen | A citizen of the internet; someone actively involved in online communities. |
| Techno-crime | Premeditated acts against systems for financial gain or information theft. |
| Techno-vandalism | Intentional disruption or damage to systems for fun or trouble. |
