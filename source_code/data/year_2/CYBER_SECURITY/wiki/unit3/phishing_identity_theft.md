# Phishing and Identity Theft

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit3

## Definition
Phishing refers to an attempt to steal sensitive information (usernames, passwords, credit card numbers) by masquerading as a reputable source.

## Key points
- Working steps: Planning (spamming), Setup (creating fake pages), Attack (sending msg), Collection (recording info), Identity theft/fraud.
- Types: Email phishing, Spear phishing (specific targets), Whaling (CEO fraud), Smishing (SMS), Vishing (Voice), Angler phishing (Social media).
- Identity theft involves stealing personal info (Name, SSN, Credit card) to commit fraud or impersonate the victim.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Spear Phishing | Targeted phishing against specific high-privilege individuals. |
| Whaling | Phishing targeting high-profile employees like CEOs. |

## Exam questions likely on this topic
- Explain the working mechanism of a phishing attack.
- Differentiate between phishing, spear phishing, and whaling.
