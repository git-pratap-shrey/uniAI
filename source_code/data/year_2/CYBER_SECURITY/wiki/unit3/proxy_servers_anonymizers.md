# Proxy Servers and Anonymizers

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit3

## Definition
A proxy server is a computer on a network which acts as an intermediary for connection with other computers on that network, acting as a privacy shield between a client and the internet.

## Key points
- Proxy servers hide the real IP address, making it harder for websites to track online activity.
- They act as a barrier to protect from harmful websites or content.
- Anonymizers (VPNs, Proxy Servers, Anonymous mail, The Onion Router) make internet activity untraceable.
- Advantages: Anonymity, security (filtering traffic), and performance (caching).
- Disadvantages: Lack of encryption, limited protocol support, and potential for logging/leaking personal data.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Anonymizer | A tool that makes internet activity untraceable by hiding source identifying information. |
| Logging | The process of recording online activities and storing them on a server. |
