# Keyloggers and Spyware

**Subject**: CYBER_SECURITY | **Unit**: unit3

## Definition
Spyware is malware that collects information about users without their knowledge; a Keylogger is a specific type of spyware that records every keystroke.

## Key points
- Keyloggers can be software-based (malicious programs) or hardware-based (physical devices between keyboard and PC).
- Spyware types: Keyloggers, Adware, Trojans, Rootkits, Browser hijackers, Web beacons.
- Anti-keyloggers are used to detect and prevent keystroke recording.
- Keyloggers capture sensitive data like passwords, credit card numbers, and personal messages.

## Important terms
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Keylogger | Software/hardware that records every key pressed on a keyboard. |
| Rootkit | Malware that infiltrates devices deeply, often as an administrator. |

## Exam questions likely on this topic
- Differentiate between Spyware and Keyloggers.
- Explain the types of keyloggers and how to protect against them.
